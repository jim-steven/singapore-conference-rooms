FAQs

Travel

How do I book my flights? +
Book through Navan using your company account. Flights should arrive by Sunday Feb 22 and depart Saturday Feb 28 or later.
Do I need a passport for Singapore? +
A valid passport is required for entry into Singapore. Ensure your passport does not expire within 6 months of our travel dates (Feb 22-27, 2026), or you may be denied entry. If renewal is needed, start immediately as processing can take several weeks. Amp teammates are responsible for any passport renewal costs.
Do I need to fill out an arrival card? +
Yes! Complete the Singapore Arrival Card (SGAC) online within 3 days before arrival. When filling out the form, select "Others" for accommodation type. Keep your confirmation email handy for immigration.
How do I get from the airport to the hotel? +
We recommend using Grab (similar to Uber). The ride from Changi Airport to Fraser Suites takes about 20-30 minutes and costs around S$20-30. Ride pooling is encouraged! Check the Travel page for teammate arrival times to coordinate rides.
Should I get a Singapore eSIM? +
Recommended! Get an eSIM for data before you arrive. Popular options: Airalo, Holafly, or Nomad. A 5-7 day plan with 3-5GB is usually sufficient. The apartments have 1 Gbps Wi-Fi, so eSIM is mainly for transit and outings.
