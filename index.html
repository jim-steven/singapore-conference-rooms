Travel

How do I book my flights? +
Book through Navan using your company account. Flights should arrive by Sunday Feb 22 and depart Saturday Feb 28 or later.
Do I need a visa for Singapore? +
Most nationalities can enter Singapore visa-free for up to 90 days. Check with the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority for your specific country.
How do I get from the airport to the hotel? +
We recommend using Grab (similar to Uber). The ride from Changi Airport to the city center takes about 20-30 minutes and costs around S$20-30.
