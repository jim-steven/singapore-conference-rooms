FAQs

Travel

How do I book my flights? +
Book through Navan using your company account. Flights should arrive by Sunday Feb 22 and depart Saturday Feb 28 or later.
Do I need a visa for Singapore? +
Most nationalities can enter Singapore visa-free for up to 90 days. Check with the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority for your specific country.
Do I need to fill out an arrival card? +
Yes! Complete the Singapore Arrival Card online before arriving.
How do I get from the airport to the hotel? +
We recommend using Grab (similar to Uber). The ride from Changi Airport to Fraser Suites takes about 20-30 minutes and costs around S$20-30.

Accommodation

Where will we be staying? +
Fraser Suites Singapore, 491B River Valley Road #16-01 Valley Point, Singapore 248373. Check the Accommodation section above for room assignments.
What time is check-in and check-out? +
Check-in is at 2pm and check-out is at 12pm. One night charge applies for early arrival or late check-out.
Is there a gym? +
Yes, the property has a gym available for all guests.
Is there a washer/dryer? +
Yes, each apartment has its own washer/dryer in-unit.

Policies

What is the cancellation policy? +
For bookings less than 1 month: 48 hours cancellation notice required, otherwise one night charge applies.
Can I extend my stay? +
For bookings less than 1 month: Extension requests are subject to availability and rate changes.
